About

CultureCall is a website where you can use meaningless "points" to bet on celebrities, events, etc. It is composed of markets, which function much like stock markets.
Markets can be created and must have a corresponding wikipedia page to prevent spam. They never "settle" and are merely bets on the future, much like stocks.
When you buy or sell a market, you put a "limit price." This means the worst price you want to buy or sell something for. We don't offer market orders as they can often not close like the price of a market due to low liquidity.
